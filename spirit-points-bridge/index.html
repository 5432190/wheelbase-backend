The business model

Do not convert points. Convert the travel plan.

Most loyalty programs restrict selling, bartering, or transferring points outside their official rules. This product is built around a safer position: customers use their own accounts, and Spirit Points Bridge sells route intelligence, alerts, and concierge booking support.

We connect the Spirit leg to other low-cost airlines, buses, trains, rental cars, or airport shuttles.

03

Deliver the booking plan

Travelers receive a step-by-step itinerary they book themselves, keeping account control and program compliance.
